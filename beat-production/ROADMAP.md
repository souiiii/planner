# Music (folder: beat-production)

> **Role:** domain roadmap for music-making as a craft. Mainly rap, with some melodic attempts.
> **Authority:** subordinate to [MASTER_ROADMAP.md](../MASTER_ROADMAP.md) for time. This file does not turn the craft into a career plan.
> **Status:** skeleton
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-27
> **Note:** the folder name is historical. The stream is broader than beat-making. Decision D-021.

## Purpose

Learn to make music well enough that you respect the results, and finish it: vocals included, not only instrumentals. Beat-making, sampling, and chopping still matter, but they are one part of the larger goal. This is a personal passion. Finished music matters more than tutorials. Decisions D-011 and D-021. Intent: [context/GOALS.md](../context/GOALS.md).

## In scope

Unordered craft areas. Not a course outline.

- Beat-making, sampling, and chopping
- Drums, bass, arrangement
- Recording vocals, mainly rap, with some melodic attempts
- Vocal processing and vocal mixing
- Beat tweaking and arrangement around a vocal
- Integrating vocals and beat into a finished record
- Making full songs over existing beats, which counts as valid progress
- Mixing, at the level the music needs
- Ear development
- Finishing pieces

## Out of scope

- Career framing, audience growth, content calendars, monetization
- Collecting plugins and watching tutorials as a substitute for finished music
- Gear shopping as progress. No gear-buying requirement
- Letting this track rewrite life priorities. Attention comes from the master roadmap
