## Purpose

Learn to make sampled beats well enough that you respect the results. This is a personal passion. Finished beats matter more than tutorials. Decision D-011. Intent: [context/GOALS.md](../context/GOALS.md).

Unordered craft areas. Not a course outline.

- Drums

- Mixing, at the level beats need
- Ear development
- Finishing pieces
