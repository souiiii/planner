# Reviews

> **Role:** where non-sprint reviews go.
> **Sprint reviews do not go here.** They live beside the plan: `sprints/sprint-NNN/REVIEW.md`.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-27

| Kind | Path | Template |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic | `reviews/strategic/YYYY-MM-DD.md` | [STRATEGIC_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md](../templates/STRATEGIC_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md) |
| Monthly | `reviews/monthly/YYYY-MM.md` | [MONTHLY_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md](../templates/MONTHLY_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md) |

Create the `strategic/` or `monthly/` directory when the first review of that kind is written. Do not pre-create empty reviews.

A strategic review's minutes are not a second master roadmap. The strategy that results goes in [MASTER_ROADMAP.md](../MASTER_ROADMAP.md). The minutes say what changed, what was assumed, and what stayed TBD.

A monthly review checks drift. It does not rewrite strategy. If strategy is wrong, it escalates. Procedure: [AI_WORKFLOW.md](../AI_WORKFLOW.md).

Do not keep a second list of reviews in this file. The directories are the index.
