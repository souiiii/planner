## 3. Product, SaaS, and independent income

- Folder: [product/](../product/ROADMAP.md)
- Intent: before joining, attempt to build at least one independent source of software income.
- Why it is here: income and the skill of taking something to people who might pay, not portfolio theater.
- Shape you already prefer: small, focused, obvious utility. Desktop utilities, browser extensions, micro-SaaS, creator tools, developer tools, workflow tools are all acceptable. None are chosen.
- Skills inside the goal: problem discovery, validation, shipping, pricing, distribution, paying users, iteration.
- Bias: fast experiments. Not six months of private building.
- What it is not: "do projects", a venture-scale startup by default, or a requirement to quit the LSEG path.
- Success shape: at least one serious attempt that reaches real users or a clear, evidence-based kill. Revenue targets are **TBD** and may stay unset.
