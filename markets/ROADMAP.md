# Markets

> **Role:** domain roadmap for practical market understanding.

> **Status:** skeleton
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-27

## Purpose

Build a strong practical understanding of how financial markets work. Personal interest is a sufficient reason. It is also relevant to joining LSEG, without assuming which team you will join. Intent: [context/GOALS.md](../context/GOALS.md).

This is a slow-burn learning track. It should not turn into another exam syllabus.

## In scope

Unordered. Not a sequence.

- Equities, bonds, rates, FX, derivatives
- Market structure
- Clearing and settlement
- Market data
- Risk

"Practical" means you can explain what the pieces are for and how they connect, including where LSEG-shaped businesses sit (venues, data, clearing, benchmarks, and similar). It does not mean a license, a trading book, or a forecast record.

## Out of scope

- Day-trading practice, signals, and get-rich material
- A professional certification program, unless you later ask for one
- Pretending to know your future LSEG business line
- A reading list the model wishes you would follow. Chosen reading goes in [READING.md](READING.md) only after you accept it
