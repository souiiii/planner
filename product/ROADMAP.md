> **Status:** skeleton
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-27

## Purpose

Before LSEG joining, make a real attempt at one independent source of software income. The point is discovery, validation, shipping, pricing, distribution, paying users, and iteration — not a pile of unfinished repos. Intent: [context/GOALS.md](../context/GOALS.md).

## In scope

- Small products with obvious utility
- Acceptable shapes, none chosen: desktop utilities, browser extensions, micro-SaaS, creator tools, developer tools, workflow tools
- Fast experiments with a kill criterion
- Using the stack you already know, unless a product genuinely requires something else

## Out of scope

- A six-month build with no outside evidence
- Venture-scale planning, pitch decks, and company cosplay, unless a specific experiment needs a one-page price and a way to get paid
- "Build projects for the portfolio" with no user contact
- Filling [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md) with ideas you did not have

Intent only.

- Evidence from people who are not you
