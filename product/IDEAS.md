# Product ideas

> **Role:** inbox for uncommitted ideas.
> **Not a roadmap,** and not a backlog of work you owe. The discovery method is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
> **Rule:** do not add an idea the owner did not state or accept. Decision D-006.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-29

An idea becomes real only when it is promoted to an experiment in [EXPERIMENTS.md](EXPERIMENTS.md) or named as a sprint outcome. Until then it can be deleted without ceremony.

## How to record one

```text
### I-NNN — name
- Date:
- Problem, in one sentence:
- Who has it:
- Outside signal (where you saw it, who, what they do today):
- Why it might be small enough:
- Status: inbox | promoted to E-NNN | dropped
```

Ids are never reused.
